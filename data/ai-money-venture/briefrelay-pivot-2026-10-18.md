# BriefRelay Profitability Pivot — October 18, 2026

## Decision

BriefRelay is shifting from selling a high-trust, $750 inbox-monitoring pilot to selling a low-friction diagnostic first:

**48-Hour Lead Leak Audit — $149 flat, credited toward implementation.**

The audit is the entry product. The recurring managed service remains the expansion product.

## Why the old motion is changing

Current evidence:

- 89 outbound emails to about 40 local-service prospects
- 1 human reply, negative
- 0 sales conversations and $0 revenue
- 20 Instagram posts, but no evidence that generic social cadence has created buying intent

The original offer asked a cold prospect to trust BriefRelay with inbox access and pay $750 before seeing proof. That is too much trust and integration friction for a first transaction.

## New entry product

### 48-Hour Lead Leak Audit

Buyer: owner-led HVAC, plumbing, or roofing company with meaningful inbound quote, emergency, or service-request volume.

Price: **$149 flat**. The full $149 is credited toward implementation if the buyer upgrades within 14 days.

Input:

- a sanitized export of up to 25 recent inquiry emails or form submissions, or
- a manually supplied set of 25 redacted lead records

No live inbox connection is required for the audit.

Deliverables:

1. Ranked list of missed, delayed, or recoverable opportunities
2. Five ready-to-approve follow-up drafts
3. Response-gap and lead-leak report
4. Fifteen-minute findings call

Trust boundary:

- synthetic or customer-approved/redacted inputs only
- no outbound messages, bookings, discounts, promises, or CRM changes without approval
- no guarantee of recovered revenue

## Expansion offer

**Daily Rescue Brief**

- $750 setup
- $299/month for one approved lead source and one daily brief
- urgent-opportunity ranking
- approval-gated reply drafts
- follow-up tracker
- weekly summary

## Go-to-market change

For the next seven days, direct proof replaces generic volume:

- Research 20 hand-picked Arizona HVAC, plumbing, and roofing operators.
- Produce five personalized mini-audits using only public-facing inquiry-flow evidence and clearly labeled hypotheses.
- Offer the $149 audit by personalized email.
- Target: 3 sales conversations and 1 paid audit.
- Keep Instagram available as credibility/support, but do not treat daily posting as the primary acquisition channel.
- Stop increasing cold-email volume if the first 20 audit-focused contacts produce zero conversations; change target or channel instead.

## Success and stop rules

Success by October 25, 2026:

- at least 3 real owner/manager conversations
- at least 1 paid $149 audit
- a documented objection pattern and one testimonial/case-study candidate if delivery occurs

Stop or revise if:

- 20 qualified personalized contacts produce zero conversations
- buyers consistently reject data-sharing even with redaction/no integration
- fulfillment takes more than four hours per audit at the $149 price
