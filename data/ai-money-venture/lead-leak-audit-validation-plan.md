# 7-Day Validation Plan — 48-Hour Lead Leak Audit

## Objective

Validate whether local HVAC, plumbing, and roofing owners will pay **$149** for a 48-Hour Lead Leak Audit before investing further in the recurring service.

## Offer under test

The buyer provides a sanitized export or 25 recent inquiry emails/forms. BriefRelay delivers:

1. Ranked missed and recoverable opportunities
2. Five ready-to-approve follow-up drafts
3. A response-gap and lead-leak report
4. A 15-minute findings call

No inbox integration is required. The $149 audit fee is credited toward an optional **$750 setup + $299/month Daily Rescue Brief**, which remains approval-gated.

## 7-day targets

- **Prospect list:** 20 hand-picked local-service businesses
- **Personalized previews:** 5 short video or text audits based only on public or prospect-supplied information
- **Conversation goal:** 3 qualified owner/operator conversations
- **Revenue goal:** 1 paid $149 audit

## Prospect criteria

Prioritize owner-led HVAC, plumbing, and roofing businesses that:

- Serve a defined local market
- Have active lead forms, quote requests, or visible review volume
- Appear large enough to receive regular inquiries but small enough for response coverage to be inconsistent
- Have a reachable owner, general manager, or operations lead
- Show a specific, truthful opportunity for a personalized observation

## Daily execution

### Day 1 — Build the list

Select 20 prospects and record trade, location, decision-maker, contact path, why the audit may fit, and one personalization signal. Exclude current duplicates and poor-fit businesses.

### Day 2 — Create five previews

Prepare five concise video or text audits. Use public observations only unless a prospect has supplied data. Do not claim access to inbox performance or invent missed leads.

### Days 3–4 — Direct outreach

Contact the 20 prospects with a short, personalized note centered on the $149 audit, 48-hour turnaround, no-integration input, and concrete deliverables. Ask for a conversation or a paid audit—not a vague expression of interest.

### Day 5 — Follow up

Send one specific follow-up to non-responders. For engaged prospects, answer objections and offer the 15-minute findings-call format. Keep all claims factual and all customer-facing drafts approval-gated.

### Day 6 — Run conversations

Use conversations to test urgency, input comfort, deliverable value, price, and credit-toward-implementation appeal. Ask what would stop the buyer from purchasing now.

### Day 7 — Close and decide

Ask qualified prospects to purchase the audit. Summarize results and decide whether to continue, revise, or stop the offer.

## Channel decision

Pause generic Instagram posting as the primary growth channel during this validation. Posting may continue only as support—for credibility, examples, or direct follow-up—not as a substitute for hand-picked outreach and buyer conversations.

## Scorecard

Track:

- 20 prospects selected
- Outreach sent and replies received
- 5 personalized previews completed
- Conversations booked/completed
- Paid audits purchased
- Objections by category
- Time required per preview and audit

## Decision rule

- **Validated:** At least 3 qualified conversations and 1 paid audit.
- **Promising but incomplete:** Conversations occur but no purchase; revise the offer once using the strongest repeated objection, then retest directly.
- **Not validated:** Fewer than 3 qualified conversations after all 20 personalized contacts and follow-ups; stop broad promotion and reassess segment, pain, or offer.
