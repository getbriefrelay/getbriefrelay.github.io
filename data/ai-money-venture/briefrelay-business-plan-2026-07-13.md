## Executive Summary

BriefRelay is an approval-gated missed-lead desk for local service businesses. It helps owners and dispatchers stop urgent requests from getting buried in email, contact forms, voicemail transcripts, and follow-up piles.

The first commercial focus is a small pilot for roofing, plumbing, HVAC, and similar local service operators in Mesa / East Valley. The promise is intentionally narrow: one approved lead source, urgent lead flags, safe reply drafts for approval, follow-up tracking, and a short daily owner brief.

BriefRelay should move forward as a service-first pilot before becoming a heavier software product. The near-term goal is not feature breadth. The goal is to prove that owners will pay for a calm, trustworthy desk that puts high-value leads and next actions back on top.

## Customer And Problem

### Initial ICP

Local service businesses where one missed request can be worth hundreds or thousands of dollars:

- Roofing companies handling inspections, leaks, storm damage, replacements, and insurance-related work.
- Plumbing companies handling same-day, burst-pipe, drain, water-heater, and emergency requests.
- HVAC companies handling no-cooling, replacement quote, seasonal emergency, and same-day calls.
- Secondary expansion: remodeling, electrical, pest control, restoration, garage door, landscaping, and other local trades with high-value inbound requests.

Best early buyer profile:

- Owner-led or small management team.
- Enough lead flow to feel inbox pain, but not so enterprise that they already have mature intake automation.
- Uses email/contact forms/voicemail transcripts heavily.
- Publicly advertises emergency, same-day, inspection, quote, or appointment workflows.
- Cares about response time but does not want reckless automation contacting customers without review.

### Core Pain

Service-business leads arrive messy. Urgent requests can sit next to vendor emails, admin threads, newsletters, voicemail transcripts, old quote conversations, and "reply after this job" tasks.

The buyer is not careless. The inbox is simply a poor operating surface for revenue triage.

BriefRelay's memorable frame:

**The inbox is where revenue goes to hide. BriefRelay puts the hot leads back on top.**

## Offer

### Pilot Offer

**7-day Missed Lead Intake Pilot**

Price anchor: **$750 flat pilot**

Scope:

- Monitor one approved lead source.
- Flag urgent or high-value requests.
- Draft safe replies for owner approval.
- Track follow-ups due.
- Send one short owner brief per day.
- Summarize what was found, what needs action, and what can wait.

Trust boundary:

- No customer messages send without approval.
- No bookings, dispatches, deletions, discounts, guarantees, or promises happen without approval.
- Drafts must clearly avoid claiming action has already been taken.

Expansion path:

- $300-$1,000/month for ongoing light managed desk support, depending on lead source count, daily volume, and follow-up complexity.
- Add-on paths after pilot validation: multi-source monitoring, CRM handoff, weekly pipeline summary, missed-call transcript review, and approved-template reply workflows.

## Positioning And Brand

BriefRelay should feel fun, friendly, funny, polished, and buyer-safe. The joke is inbox chaos, never the owner, customer, or service work.

Brand system:

- Active identity: V4D Lead Lift.
- Visual direction: bold work-artifact system with urgent lead cards, approval stamps, owner briefs, missed-call slips, dispatch notes, and service-business cues.
- Tone: warm, direct, lightly funny, operationally useful.

Messaging pillars:

- Hot leads should not need a search party.
- Approval-gated help, not autopilot promises.
- One clean daily brief beats one messy inbox dig.
- Follow-ups stop living in someone's head.
- Service-business owners need calm triage, not more dashboards.
